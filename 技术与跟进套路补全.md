## initial

### 一句话先回答，再分项展开

你的首轮回复通常先引用或概述 W/Q，随后用一句话确定回答方向，再用编号小标题展开。一个问题包含数个子问题时，先写 “We address the question in three parts for clarity”，再将 Q2 拆成 Q2-1、Q2-2、Q2-3，按同样顺序给答案。

### baseline 与公平比较

共性动作是 **肯定比较价值 → 解释用户面临的选择 → 匹配预算/模型系列/设置 → 展示结果 → 讨论权衡**。LoRAM 从 resource-constrained user perspective 解释大小模型比较；FloE 增加较小非压缩模型；HybridKV 匹配 KV budget 后同时报告质量与 latency。

```text
Comparing with [baseline] is important for assessing [claim]. Our setup
reflects the practical choice between [A] and [B] under [resource budget].
To make this comparison meaningful, we match [settings] and report both
[quality metric] and [cost metric]. The results in [table] show [finding].
This supports [specific value] for [target scenario], while [remaining
trade-off] is acknowledged.
```

### 不回避性能代价，解释为什么仍有价值

LoRAM 明确承认同规模 LoRA 是性能上界；HybridKV 明确承认相对某些静态方法有额外 latency，再解释质量与场景收益。这种“承认局部代价 → 说明机制 → 对目标用户推荐”的论证，比声称各方面全面领先更贴近你的原文。

```text
Certainly, [method] trades [some quality / additional overhead] for
[resource benefit]. As shown in [table], [specific comparison].
The difference arises from [mechanism]. For [target users/settings],
we believe this trade-off is worthwhile because [validated practical value].
```

### 分开一次性成本与用户反复承担的成本

LoRAM 将 alignment 写成 model publisher 可做的一次性 offline 工作，将用户 online low-rank fine-tuning 单独评价；随后给 peak memory、latency、throughput。FloE 给 predictor 绝对耗时与 block 耗时作分母；HybridKV 将总 latency 拆成 KV selection、loading、attention 等组成。

```text
We separate two costs: [one-time preparation] and [per-use operation].
The former is performed by [actor] on [data/workload]; the latter is
measured under [settings]. We report [absolute metrics] in [table].
For [component], the measured overhead is [time], compared with [time]
for [clearly defined denominator].
```

### 对“结果趋势为什么相反”按阶段拆解

LoRAM 的回复比较了 before recovery 与 after recovery，先说明前者与已有结论一致，再解释后者发生变化的机制。HybridKV 的 all-dynamic 消融则分开 accuracy 风险与效率成本。你经常将一个笼统负面结论拆成不同条件下的不同判断。

```text
It helps to distinguish [stage/condition A] from [stage/condition B].
Under [A], [result], consistent with [reference/baseline]. Under [B],
[different result]. We attribute this difference to [mechanism], supported
by [evidence]. We will make this distinction explicit in [location].
```

### 参数选择的三层回答

**设计目标/取舍 → 已有敏感性分析或补充实验 → 适用范围和用户可调性**。LoRAM 解释不寻找 universally optimal ratio，而是服务内存约束；HybridKV 解释阈值两端的风险并给验证集选择；LVSpec 给 N 与 λ 的趋势，并说明何时需要调整。

```text
The choice of [parameter] is guided by [design objective]. A value that is
too [low] leads to [risk], whereas one that is too [high] leads to [cost].
The sensitivity analysis in [location] evaluates this trade-off.
Our default is [value] under [conditions]; users may adjust it according
to [resource/quality constraint].
```

### 资源限制之后给替代证据

FloE 明确说未能自行 distill 小模型，改为比较已有的小模型；LVSpec 写清测试子集、模型 pairing、seeds。学习这里的坦率语气和可执行替代方式，不把“未做原实验”隐藏起来。

```text
Due to [time/resource constraint], we could not complete a reliable
evaluation of [requested experiment]. We instead evaluate [alternative]
under [settings], which addresses [specific aspect] of the concern.
The remaining question is [boundary], which our current evidence does
not establish.
```

### scope、可迁移原则与 corner case

FloE 将 memory-centric 与 compute-utilization 问题分开；LVSpec 解释 visual grounding 范围并给无关视频测试与 fallback；HybridKV 将当前分类方法的范围与可迁移的 head-heterogeneity 原则分开。这是**本文要解决什么 → 为什么这么定义 → 边界如何表现 → 可以怎样扩展**。

```text
Our study focuses on [problem and setting]. The case you raise concerns
[different setting / deployment boundary]. We are happy to discuss it.
Under [evaluated case], [result]. Beyond this setting, [adaptation/fallback]
may be needed. We will clarify this scope and the remaining limitation
in [location].
```

### 写作、符号、相关工作：给可核查的修订承诺

LVSpec 将 contribution 旧表述和拟改新表述逐字列出，给出 notation shapes 的澄清位置；LoRAM 对 typo 写出 0.3415 → 34.15，并说明修订稿蓝色标记；HybridKV 先概述建议文献，再说明如何与本文比较。小修也用“动作＋位置”回答。

```text
Thank you for pointing this out. We will revise “[old wording]” to
“[new wording]” in [location]. We will also [specific related change].
[If completed: The correction is included in version dated X and marked
in Y for ease of reference.]
```

## followup

### 新结果：日期标记与回应闭环

LoRAM 的 Update 11.23 和 Update 11.24、LVSpec 的 Update 2.20 / 2.21 都把后续结果写进原问题对应的回复。固定结构是 **日期＋主题 → 为什么补 → 设置 → 表格/结果 → 回答原 concern → 修订位置**。时间先后可说明作者确实持续推进；以当前版本阅读时不要把后加内容当作最初已提交。

```text
Update [date] — [Specific topic]

We are excited to share the additional results on [topic]. To address
[original concern], we evaluated [models/data/settings]. As shown in
[table], [result]. This supports [bounded conclusion]. We have added
[analysis] to [appendix/section], where revisions are permitted.
```

### 代码：可用性、支持与自信推荐

你的 LoRAM 回复先报告匿名代码已上传，坦率说明初版的限制，再承诺解答使用问题，最后向资源受限用户推荐并欢迎反馈。FloE 的代码回应也把开放实现与社区使用连接起来。

```text
We are delighted to share that [implementation] is now available through
[venue-permitted location]. This version [accurate current status].
We are happy to clarify any implementation details and address issues
you may encounter. For [target users], we believe [method] is worth
considering because [validated benefit]. Your feedback and suggestions
are always welcome.
```

### reviewer 条件式反馈：逐项落实，然后报告结果

LoRAM BSMa 先提出术语和 unchanged weights 的明确要求；作者回复分别给修改与 Appendix C.5 的位置；reviewer 随后明确提分。提分后作者的感谢仍提及论文改进和落实讨论内容。

```text
Thank you for clarifying the remaining issues. We have addressed them as
follows: [issue A → change and location; issue B → evidence and location].
We hope these updates help assess [specific concern], and we welcome any
remaining feedback.
```

### 给一个 reviewer 的阶段小结

LoRAM 还单独写了 “To Sum Up of Response to Reviewer asc9”，将 W1 创新性、W2 pruning、W3 实验分析汇为一个短导航，然后请求进一步讨论。这是面向 reviewer 的小结，与给 AC 的期末 Summary 不同。

```text
To facilitate further discussion, we summarize our responses below:
1. [Concern A]: [answer and evidence location].
2. [Concern B]: [answer and evidence location].
3. [Concern C]: [answer and evidence location].
We welcome further discussion of any point that remains unresolved.
```

## reminders

### 轻量提醒与最后一轮实质跟进

你的轻量提醒使用 finds you well → recent exchange → approaching deadline → remaining concerns → looking forward → Best regards。最后一轮的 LVSpec / Survey 则增加一组与原 W 对应的更新，再以作者视角强调贡献，条件式邀请更新评价。

**最后一轮的完整结构：**截止提醒 → concern—response 清单 → 对贡献的自信 → 若疑问解决则请求重新评价 → 愿意继续回答 → 感谢。

```text
Title: Follow-up on [Main Concern] — Discussion Closing on [Deadline]

Dear Reviewer [ID],

As the discussion enters its final stage, we would like to summarize our
responses to the points you raised:
1. [W1]: [clarification/evidence; location].
2. [W2]: [additional experiment; result and location].
3. [W3]: [revision or acknowledged scope].

We are proud that these updates strengthen [specific contribution].
If they resolve the concerns underlying your assessment, we would
appreciate an updated evaluation reflecting the discussion. We remain
happy to address any remaining questions before [deadline].

Thank you again for your time and insights.
Best regards,
The Authors
```

### 缺少评审与缺少反馈分开请求

LVSpec 首次 AC 评论写 only one review，先体谅招募困难再请求更多评审；SAC 回复协调 emergency reviews。后续的“已有 reviewers 不回应”则请求促进讨论。两者都需要清楚写出人数、时间和希望 chairs 做的动作。

```text
Dear Area Chair,

Thank you for organizing the review process. We understand the difficulty
of recruiting reviewers within a limited timeframe. At present, [N]
review(s) are available for our submission. We would appreciate your
guidance on [additional review / sufficient opportunity to respond], so
that the work can receive a comprehensive assessment.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
Best regards,
The Authors
```

## reports

### 先普通保密反馈，再按正式窗口报告

Survey 有 Formal Feedback 与后续 Review Issue Report；LVSpec 有 late review 反馈与逐 W 对应证据的 AC 保密帖。它们共同使用“称呼 chairs → 指出具体问题 → 分项论证 → 影响 → 请求综合判断 → 署名”。正式表单的类别以本轮选项为准，不能只复制旧编号。

### 原句要保留，但区分作者主张与已经核实的事实

原帖的 “Contradiction …”“remained silent …”“resolved concerns” 是作者的论证。教学时可以展示原来的语气与请求力度；可套用版本明确区分：文本/时间能证明什么，作者怎样解释，请 chairs 核查什么。特别是沉默不能直接证明认可，低分也不能单凭人数推翻。

### 迟到 review 的独立模板

```text
The new review became available on [time and time zone], after the author
response window closed on [time and time zone]. The new concerns are
[topics], and we currently have no authorized reply mechanism.
We request an opportunity to provide a concise response before these
concerns are used in the final assessment. Please advise on the appropriate
channel and deadline.
```

## recommend

### 对社区、对用户与对评审的三种自信

**对社区：**用工作可以引出的研究方向表达价值；**对用户：**指明适用任务与资源约束，邀请实际使用和反馈；**对评审：**把贡献和已验证证据汇成正面评价请求。三个对象不同，措辞也不同。

在你给出的五个原始讨论页中，commitment 阶段完整的 response-to-meta-review 不能一概从 ARR 讨论反推。手册保留这一阶段的派生模板，并标明不是五篇共同原句。
