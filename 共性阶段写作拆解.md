## 先看全周期

这份手册按“要发什么帖子”组织。每一阶段都放在同一个位置讲清：什么时候发、谁能看、段落顺序、原帖截图、可复用表达和完整模板。先选阶段，再照骨架填事实。

| 顺序 | 阶段 | 这封回复要完成什么 | 内容骨架 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 逐条 Initial Response | 正面回答每个 W/Q | 对应原问题 → 直接回答 → 解释 → 证据 → 修改位置 |
| 2 | General Response | 将评审认可与本轮更新汇总成整体印象 | Reviewer 映射 → 感谢 → 按主题归纳认可 → 过渡 → 贡献 → 实验更新 → 讨论更新 → 收尾 |
| 3 | Follow-up / 提分后回复 | 回应新疑问，落实修改，维持对话 | 接住反馈 → 定位残留问题 → 新解释/修改 → 请求确认；提分后则感谢并落实承诺 |
| 4 | 催 reviewer | 在窗口内争取剩余反馈 | 提及已提交回应 → 提醒讨论截止 → 询问剩余 concern → 表达愿意继续回应 |
| 5 | 请 AC 协调 / 催 AC | 让 chairs 知道具体需要协调什么 | 感谢主持 → 已完成回应 → 哪位尚缺反馈 → 截止影响 → 具体请求 |
| 6 | Summary of the Discussion Period | 让 AC 看清贡献、讨论结果与剩余分歧 | 感谢 AC → 汇总认可 → 已做工作 → 提分/反馈 → 分 reviewer 状态 → 正面推荐 → 收尾 |
| 7 | 私下反馈 / Review Issue Report | 用可核查事实指出评审问题 | 问题类型 → 原话与时间 → 原稿/回复证据 → 影响 → 处理请求 |

**时间与权限卡：**先记下初始回复截止 B、回复实际向 reviewer 开放的时间 V、作者互动截止 D、问题报告窗口，以及字符/次数/改稿限制。催审从 V 开始判断。具体按钮和 Readers 服从当轮会议，历史案例用于学习写法。

## 重点标注与组合格式

你的排版有明确的分工：**加粗负责让人先扫到判断与证据，斜体提炼被认可的特点，彩色行内代码标出需要找到的位置或对象。**组合后，reviewer 和 AC 能先抓住“你希望他记住什么”，再看到依据和定位。

### 先辨认语法

你截图里呈现为彩色文字的是**行内代码样式**，使用两侧的反引号（backtick，字符 `），不是普通单引号（字符 '）。例如下面源代码中的 General Response。

```text
**加粗**
*斜体*
`General Response`
**`Section 4.3`**
***加粗并斜体***
```

行内代码在你的 OpenReview 截图中呈红色/棕红色；这是站点样式带来的显示效果，不是 Markdown 自定义文字颜色的命令。普通的 'text' 不会产生这个效果。会议需启用 Markdown，发布前看预览；本手册的彩色效果只是示意。[OpenReview 官方格式说明](https://docs.openreview.net/how-to-guides/submissions-comments-reviews-and-decisions/how-to-add-formatting-to-reviews-or-comments)

### 从原帖总结每种格式的作用

| 格式 | 你的常见用法 | 给 reviewer / AC 的阅读信号 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 加粗 | W/Q 原问题、分论点标题、核心结论、关键机制、模型、数字、R 编号 | 先看这些就能抓住问题、回答和依据 |
| 斜体 | 意义、方法特点、实验覆盖维度、对论文的正面评价 | 从较长的认可段中提取“认可了什么” |
| 行内代码的彩色样式 | General Response、Section 4.3、Related Work section、具体 appendix | 这里是要回看的回复、章节或明确对象 |
| 加粗 + 斜体内容与 R 编号相邻 | *simplicity and effectiveness* **(R2, R5)** | 评价词与评价来源一起被看见 |
| 加粗 + 行内代码 | **`Section 4.3`** | 同时强调“要改到哪里 / 去哪里查” |
| 段落标题 + 编号 + 局部加粗 | 分论点名称、1/2/3、关键短语 | 把完整回答变成可扫描的逻辑层次 |

特别注意：**斜体特点 + 加粗 R 编号**通常是两个相邻的标注，分别强调内容和归属；它不必把整段都做成粗斜体。粗斜体可作为额外组合，但不是这组截图中每一处都使用的固定做法。

### 组合一 General Response 的认可段

**原帖模式：评价内容用斜体，评价来源用加粗。**截图里的 LoRAM 就把 simplicity and effectiveness、实验维度和写作优点逐项处理，并分别标注 R 编号。

**示意效果，非原帖逐字摘录：**

We appreciate the reviewers' recognition of the *importance of our research problem* **(R1, R2, R5)** and the *simplicity and effectiveness of our method* **(R2, R5)**. We also value their acknowledgment of our *evaluation across model scales and tasks* **(R1, R2, R3)**.

**可复制 Markdown：**

```text
We appreciate the reviewers' recognition of the *importance of our research
problem* **(R1, R2, R5)** and the *simplicity and effectiveness of our method*
**(R2, R5)**. We also value their acknowledgment of our *evaluation across
model scales and tasks* **(R1, R2, R3)**.
```

### 怎么安排阅读重点

每段先确定一个你希望读者记住的点，再决定格式：**要记住的回答加粗，支持评价的特点用斜体，要查证的位置用代码样式。**关键数据应同时附设置和单位，不只突出最好看的数字。全文通读后，再只看加粗部分：它们应能串起问题、贡献、证据和请求。

如果一句话全部加粗，内部重点就消失了；如果每个普通词都上色，读者也难以分辨“结论”与“定位”。你的常见做法是保留普通正文承载解释，让局部标注承担导航。

## 逐条回复的共性格式

**什么时候：**初始截止前。**发哪里：**对应 review 的指定回复入口。**谁能看：**按该会议机制，可能同稿其他 reviewers 也能看；不能默认只有被回复者可见。

### 从你的原帖提炼出的结构

| 层次 | 写什么 | 你的常见写法 | 可替换内容 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 标题 | Reviewer 与问题定位 | Thanks & Initial Response to Reviewer [ID] (W1,W2) | 同一主题可合并 W/Q；不默认允许拆帖 |
| 原问题 | 短引或准确概述批评 | [W1 – …] / [Suggestion 1] / [Question 1] | 保留决定性限定，避免复制整份 review |
| 接话 | 回应具体问题 | “We agree that … is important.” | 只对认同的部分使用 agree |
| 主回答 | 第一两句给立场 | “Our work primarily focuses on …” | 是/否、已有/新增、范围差异 |
| 展开 | 用有意义的小标题分论点 | Theoretical Grounding / Empirical Validation | 不把所有问题都硬拆成 theory + experiment |
| 证据 | 原稿位置、实验设置、表格 | “As detailed in Appendix D …” | 标明原稿已有还是本轮新增 |
| 落地 | 具体修改位置 | “We will include these results in Section 4.3.” | 已完成与拟修改分开 |

### 最常用的回应路径

- **事实误读：**澄清定义 → 原稿位置 → 为什么改变对该问题的判断。
- **创新性不足：**已有工作的目标 → 本文观察 → 本文机制 → 结果与实际价值。LoRAM 和 HybridKV 都采用过 empirical innovation / technical innovation 的分法。
- **实验不够：**承认比较价值 → 匹配设置 → 补充结果 → 解释收益与代价 → 修改位置。
- **范围/局限：**定义主张边界 → 解释边界由来 → 给已有或允许补充的证据 → 说明 fallback 或后续方向。

## 回应 novelty 质疑的共性套路

这套套路最明确地重复出现在 **LoRAM 的 EtZ8 / asc9 回复和 HybridKV 的 mmBU W2 回复**。不是五篇都被问了同样的 novelty 问题；这里提炼的是实际重复使用的论证结构。

### 主套路：先拆创新维度，再逐项证明

| 顺序 | 你的论证动作 | 原帖中实际采用的写法 | 作用 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 接住 novelty 质疑 | We respectfully acknowledge the reviewer’s concern. | 不急着否认，进入具体论证 |
| B | 将创新分成两条证据线 | Empirical Innovation / Technical Innovation | 让“发现新现象”和“设计新方法”都得到评价 |
| C | 解释经验创新是什么 | Uncovering new model behaviors and principles | 说明本文新增了什么认识，而非仅报更高分 |
| D | 对应到本文的新发现 | Our contributions on empirical innovation: … | 给 observation、分析位置及其意义 |
| E | 解释技术创新是什么 | Developing novel methods or optimizations … | 说明机制与能力上的新增内容 |
| F | 对应到本文的机制 | Our contributions on technical innovation: … | 让发现自然导向设计，再给效果证据 |
| G | 回到价值与验证 | contributions … validated across models, datasets, and tasks | 收束贡献及已有验证，而不是停在“我们有创新” |

**原句开头，LoRAM 与 HybridKV 均使用：**

> While novelty is a multifaceted concept in academic research, we believe it can be broadly viewed from two perspectives:

> (1) Empirical Innovation: Uncovering new model behaviors and principles …

> (2) Technical Innovation: Developing novel methods or optimizations to advance existing technologies.

原帖用 Scaling Laws、Lottery Ticket Hypothesis、LoRA 举例解释这两个维度。讲解时可以保留这个例子；实际篇幅紧张时压成一句，随后立刻转到自己论文的具体发现与技术机制。

### LoRAM 与 HybridKV 的并排对应

| 模块 | LoRAM | HybridKV |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 被质疑的点 | 看起来只是 pruning + LoRA | 看起来与 TailorKV 都是 hybrid KV compression |
| 新发现 | base model 的训练内存存在可压缩空间；剪枝损失可通过低成本 alignment 恢复 | MLLM attention heads 存在 static/dynamic 行为；prefill 指标可用于预测 decoding 行为 |
| 新机制 | 小模型训练低秩矩阵，再恢复维度并使用完整模型推理 | head classification → top-down budget allocation → head-specific compression |
| 区别的关键轴 | 训练与推理使用哪个模型、恢复的对象是什么、研究目标是什么 | MLLM vs LLM、head vs layer 粒度、何时为哪类 head 选策略 |
| 价值收束 | 减少训练内存，同时保留推理性能 | 利用 head 异质性，在质量与效率间取得所验证的收益 |

### 分支一：被说“只是组合已有组件”

你的写法是 **承认已有组件 → 明确贡献层次 → 指出新决策/机制 → 给必要性与效果证据**。承认局部复用之后，仍然明确表达创新，不把整项工作也退让掉。

**HybridKV mmBU W4 原句：**

> Our core innovation, however, is not a new compression primitive, but the novel hybrid framework that orchestrates different strategies based on an effective head classification.

**同帖进一步凝练的研究问题：**

> which heads get which type of compression

这句话把贡献定位成一个此前未被相同方式处理的策略选择问题。论证不是“组合也算创新”就结束，而是解释为何需要新分类、为什么要按 head 选择不同策略、已有工作在哪个粒度做什么。

```text
We agree that [component] builds on existing work, as acknowledged in
[location]. Our core innovation lies in [new principle/framework/decision].
Specifically, we address the question of [precise research question].
This requires [new mechanism A] and [new mechanism B], enabling [capability]
that is not provided by simply applying [existing component].
The comparison/ablation in [location] evaluates this distinction directly.
```

### 分支二：被说“和某个 related work 差不多”

你的论证顺序是 **承认相关工作的价值 → 按明确维度比较 → 说明本文新问题与机制 → 解释互补关系 → 回到直接实验比较**。

**HybridKV 原帖的小标题：**

- Distinct Scope and Granularity
- Orthogonality to TailorKV's Techniques

**原句：**

> Therefore, our work addresses a new problem space (MLLM attention heterogeneity) with a finer-grained conceptual solution.

> We consider these techniques orthogonal and complementary to our work.

“Orthogonal” 后面，你实际解释了怎样结合：TailorKV 的 quantization 可用于本文识别的 static heads，prefetching 可优化 dynamic heads 的 retrieval。**能具体说明怎么接合，才让“互补”有内容。**

```text
We recognize [prior work] as an important contribution to [area].
The distinction lies in [scope] and [granularity/mechanism].
[Prior work] addresses [problem] through [mechanism]. Our work instead
studies [specific problem] and introduces [new mechanism] to address it.
These techniques are complementary: [concrete way they could be combined].
The comparison in [location] evaluates their respective trade-offs under
[matched conditions].
```

### 分支三：把 simplicity 说成优势

**LoRAM 原句：**

> We intentionally keep the method as simple as possible, which, in our view, is more of an advantage than a drawback.

这句话后面接的是被方法揭示的新性质、资源受限用户的实际问题及实验效果。可以坚定地肯定简单，但要说明“简单而有效”的技术与使用价值。

```text
We intentionally keep the method simple. This is a practical advantage:
[low implementation burden / no training / compatibility / low overhead].
The contribution is [new insight or mechanism], and the evidence in
[location] shows that this simple design achieves [bounded result].
```

### 分支四：被继续追问时，逐条解释 first 的边界

LoRAM 后续回复将 “first to target …” 和 “first to propose …” 分成两条，分别解释**目标问题**与**训练—推理范式**的区别。不是再堆几个 novel，而是把对方引用的方法放进同一比较框架。

| 可复用句式 | 填写重点 |
| --- | --- |
| Our contributions on empirical innovation are … | 每条一个新观察 + 证据位置 |
| Our contributions on technical innovation are … | 新机制如何由观察推出 |
| The novelty of [paradigm] lies in … | 明确新在哪里，而不只写 novel paradigm |
| The distinction lies in both [axis A] and [axis B]. | 目标、对象、粒度、机制、训练/推理阶段 |
| Our primary innovation lies in shifting the perspective from [X] to [Y]. | 转换后解决了什么具体问题 |
| This insight drives our technical contributions, including … | 把发现与机制连接起来 |
| We consider these techniques orthogonal and complementary. | 随后说明实际可怎样结合 |
| We are the first to [precisely defined contribution]. | 只用于经过相关工作核对、范围准确的首次主张 |

### 完整 novelty 回复模板

```text
[Concern — The method appears to combine existing components / resembles X.]

We appreciate the opportunity to clarify the contribution. We view the
novelty of this work along two dimensions: empirical insights and technical
design.

1. Empirical innovation
We identify [new phenomenon / overlooked bottleneck], supported by
[analysis and location]. This matters because [consequence for the field
or for how the problem should be solved].

2. Technical innovation
Building on this insight, we introduce [mechanism/framework]. Specifically,
[A] enables [capability], while [B] addresses [challenge]. The evidence in

3. Distinction from the closest work
We acknowledge that [component] builds on [prior work]. The key distinction

4. Simplicity and practical significance
We intentionally keep [part of the method] simple, which allows [benefit].
Together, [new insight], [new mechanism], and [validated result] constitute

```

## 人味表达与自信邀请

你的回复不只是“感谢—辩解—给证据”。反复出现的交流方式是：**认可问题的价值，说明我们已怎样考虑，再主动、乐意地解释；也会把工作推荐给真正能受益的用户。**下面先保留原句，再给可以替换的表达。原句旁的链接能核对上下文；模板是基于这些原句整理的新句式。

### 一 乐意解释或配合

| 你的原句 | 出处 | 语气和作用 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| We are happy to provide further explanations below. | HybridKV，JEeP W2；4Xp9 W1 | 已有答案，愿意在这里展开，轻松而笃定 |
| We are happy to provide additional results for more choices of N, as shown in Table 2. | LVSpec，B2rN W1–W3 | 不只表达配合，马上给具体结果与位置 |
| … we would be happy to share additional insights into how our approach interacts with GPU performance. | FloE，u26i | 主问题之外也乐意分享，随后给测量结果 |
| … we would be more than happy to make adjustments. This would greatly help in refining our work! | LoRAM，asc9 后续回复 | 对呈现方式的具体建议保持开放，愿意配合修改 |
| Should you have any remaining concerns or require further clarification, we are willing to continue the discussion to further improve the quality of our work. | HybridKV，mmBU 后续回复 | 收到提分后仍保留对话空间 |

“We are happy to …” 是现在就愿意做、并常常紧接着给内容；“We would be happy to …” 适合提出可以做的配合；“We would be more than happy to …” 热情更强，适合具体修改或帮助，不必每段重复。

**来源：**[HybridKV JEeP W2](https://openreview.net/forum?id=jFuN2yI2D7&noteId=uJyQ8V8HPs)；[LVSpec B2rN](https://openreview.net/forum?id=xyXsaQEmCu&noteId=CBQBJuiKHd)；[FloE u26i](https://openreview.net/forum?id=i5aHAkkhJH&noteId=RNa5lFqnlJ)；[LoRAM 后续回复](https://openreview.net/forum?id=s7DkcgpRxL&noteId=XVxKzaXxQS)；[HybridKV mmBU 后续](https://openreview.net/forum?id=jFuN2yI2D7&noteId=fvJp5PEkq3)。

### 二 这个问题我们设计时就考虑过，文中已有，这里乐意再解释

这是一个很值得保留的固定动作：**肯定问题重要 → 说明设计时已有考虑 → 指向原稿 → 愉快地重述 → 给实质答案**。既体现研究准备充分，也照顾对方的阅读体验。

**HybridKV 原帖恰好就是完整例子：**

> Thank you for the valuable suggestion. We agree that providing deeper grounding for our hyperparameter choices is important. In fact, both the sparsity threshold (θ) and the budget allocation coefficient (r) are selected based on theoretical grounding and systematic empirical validation, as detailed in Appendix D. We are happy to provide further explanations below.

**LVSpec 原帖明确说设计阶段就做过：**

> In fact, we have conducted early experiments during the method design stage, and have empirically compared using last-layer hidden states versus text and visual embeddings as the relevance signal, which involves the influence of a text tokenizer, as summarized in Table 1.

**LoRAM 指向已有消融：**

> Identifying the trade-offs of "train small, infer small" is indeed crucial, and its performance implications are already reflected in the Ablation Study of our paper.

这些表达的力量在于紧接着给解释和证据。可以讲“已经考虑”，同时为 reviewer 在当前回复里重新组织答案。

**来源：**[HybridKV JEeP W2](https://openreview.net/forum?id=jFuN2yI2D7&noteId=uJyQ8V8HPs)；[LVSpec RMKs Q1–Q2](https://openreview.net/forum?id=xyXsaQEmCu&noteId=VMCeO5xlzt)；[LoRAM BSMa W1–W2](https://openreview.net/forum?id=s7DkcgpRxL&noteId=FhXEFRoaaI)。上方数学符号统一为 θ、r，正文措辞保留原帖。

### 五个槽位逐句填

| 句子功能 | 可替换表达 | 应接什么内容 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 肯定问题 | This is an important consideration. / We agree that [issue] is important. | 说出具体问题，不空泛感谢 |
| 设计时考虑过 | We considered this issue when designing [method]. | 当时的设计决定或实验 |
| 原稿已有说明 | This is discussed in Section [X] and evaluated in Table [Y] of the original submission. | 准确区分 discussed 与 evaluated |
| 乐意重述 | We are happy to revisit this point here. / We are happy to provide further explanations below. | 接下来直接给答案 |
| 实质澄清 | Specifically, [mechanism / finding / trade-off]. | 1–2 个最关键论点或数据 |

“We are happy to restate the rationale here” 是自然的推荐改写；不是上述原帖的逐字引文。“Reiterate” 更强调重申已有立场，适合 scope 或核心区别；重新解释细节时，clarify / explain / revisit 往往更柔和。

```text
Thank you for raising this important point. We considered [issue] when
designing [method], and discuss [decision/rationale] in Section [X] of
the original submission. We are happy to revisit the reasoning here.
Specifically, [direct explanation]. The results in Table [Y] show [finding],
which is why we chose [design].
```

```text
We agree that [concern] is important. In fact, this was one of the
considerations behind [design choice], as discussed in [original location].
For ease of reference, we summarize the key point here: [answer].
We are happy to clarify any part that remains unclear.
```

```text
We explored this question during the method design stage and compared
[A] with [B]. As reported in [original location], [finding].
We are happy to explain how this informed our choice of [design].
```

使用时只填真实状态：当时考虑过、当时做过实验、当时写进原稿，是三种不同事实。如果原稿未写清楚，可以自然补一句 “We should have made this rationale more explicit.”，并说明准备怎样改。

### 三 欢迎使用与推荐我们的工作

你的自信表达经常面向具体用户：指出谁可能受益、在哪种约束下有用，再邀请使用、推荐或反馈。它同时传递实用性、可用性和作者愿意支持使用者的态度。

**LoRAM 原帖：**

> If you know users with resource constraints who could benefit from task-specific customization, LoRAM is definitely worth recommending! Your feedback and suggestions are always welcome, and we deeply appreciate your engagement with our work!

此前同段还写到愿意解决用户可能遇到的问题，并继续完善更易用的实现。这使推荐带有对实际使用的支持，而非单纯的自我表扬。[LoRAM H1mX W2–W4](https://openreview.net/forum?id=s7DkcgpRxL&noteId=PYRFyPClEd)

**FloE 原帖：**

> For users with limited resources who cannot deploy MoE models, we highly recommend FloE as an efficient and practical alternative, delivering high performance while significantly reducing computational costs.

它放在 baseline 比较与结果分析之后，结构是 **证据 → 适合谁 → 为什么推荐**。[FloE bhnT 回复](https://openreview.net/forum?id=i5aHAkkhJH&noteId=kyvWCY4VKH)

### 邀请使用的句式库

下列为结合你的语气整理的推荐写法，非原帖逐字引文。

| 意图 | 自然表达 |
| --- | --- |
| 直接欢迎试用 | We would be happy for you to try [method] in [setting] and share your experience. |
| 邀请检查实现 | We welcome you to explore the implementation and would be happy to clarify any details. |

```text
For researchers working on [task] under [resource constraint], we believe
[method] offers a practical option: [validated benefit and conditions].

```

| 原帖中的表达 | 功能 |
| --- | --- |
| Should you have any additional questions or require further clarification, please do not hesitate to let us know. | LoRAM：给对方继续提问的入口 |
| … we sincerely welcome any additional feedback. | LoRAM：继续保持讨论开放 |
| … we are happy to discuss it further. | LVSpec：即使边界问题超出主范围，也愿意讨论 |

## General Response 的共性骨架

**用途：**把 review 中已经出现的认可和本轮回应组织成一个完整叙述，让所有 reviewer 与 AC 快速看清工作的价值和改进。这里的优点来自审稿人的评语，不由作者临时编写。**位置与权限：**LoRAM 是 Official Comment / Everyone；ARR 案例读者包括相应 reviewers、chairs、authors 等，需看实际 Readers。

### 八个模块按这个顺序写

| 模块 | 段落功能 | 内容从哪里来 | 常见格式 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A 编号映射 | 让后文 R1/R2 指代稳定 | 当前 reviewer ID | 一句话列 ID 与编号对应关系 |
| B 总体感谢 | 接住评审投入和整体认可 | reviews 的反馈 | 感谢 + encouraged by recognition |
| C 按维度汇总认可 | 用 reviewers 自己的评价建立贡献的整体印象 | Strengths、Summary、正面评语、后续认可 | 研究意义 → 方法/创新 → 实验 → 实用性/写作；每条带 (R1,R2) |
| D 过渡 | 从“已认可什么”转到“我们做了什么” | 本轮回应与修订 | 已逐条回答 + Below, we summarize … |
| E 核心贡献 | 用作者视角重新归纳论文贡献 | 原论文 + 已有证据 | Core Contributions of Our Work，编号列 3–4 点 |
| F 实验更新 | 汇总新增证据 | 允许新增且已完成的实验 | 更新标题 + 做了什么 + 回答哪个 concern + 结果/位置 |
| G 讨论更新 | 汇总解释、分析与范围澄清 | 逐条回复和改稿 | 机制说明、创新性、超参、局限、相关工作等 |
| H 收尾 | 说明修订状态并争取正面评价 | 真实完成状态 | 修订位置/版本 + favorable consideration + 感谢 |

五篇的变化：LoRAM 将实验和讨论更新分开；HybridKV 用两帖延续同一结构；LVSpec 将部分实验与讨论更新合并；Survey 在更新下标具体 appendix；FloE 的历史帖子把 General Response 与 Summary 合并。**共同的是功能顺序，是否分帖与是否新增结果则由当轮规则决定。**

### C 段怎么做：先摘 review，再按维度合并

先做一张素材表。优点至少保留四项：**原话、reviewer ID、优点主题、可以忠实转述的意思**。同一个 reviewer 完全可以既认可方法有效，又质疑创新性；只提炼他明确认可的那一部分。

| Review 原话 | 主题 | General Response 中的提炼 | 归属 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| “Memory efficient training is an important topic in LLM-related fields.” | 研究意义 | recognition of the importance of our research problem | LoRAM BSMa / R5 |
| “This paper proposes an easy but effective method …” | 方法简洁有效 | acknowledgment of the simplicity and effectiveness of our method | LoRAM BSMa / R5 |
| “The proposed method is complementary with other memory reduction techniques such as quantization and pruning strategies.” | 兼容性 | recognition of its compatibility with quantization and pruning strategies | LoRAM H1mX / R4 |
| “The paper is fairly well structured and well written. Visualizations are quite helpful for quickly grasping the method.” | 表达与图示 | highlighting the clear structure and helpful visualizations | LoRAM H1mX / R4 |

上述原话均可在对应 Official Review 核对。合并多个 reviewer 的评价时，**每个主题分别归属**，不要把整段末尾统一标 (R1–R5)，让人误以为每个人都说过每一个优点。

### 从你的共同写法中提炼的认可段模板

```text
In particular, we appreciate the reviewers' recognition of the importance
of [research problem], which addresses [specific challenge] ([R IDs]).
We are grateful for the acknowledgment of [method's concrete strengths]
([R IDs]), especially [specific mechanism or benefit] ([R ID]).
Furthermore, [compatibility / scalability / practical utility] was
recognized by [R IDs]. We also appreciate the positive assessment of our
experiments across [models / tasks / settings] ([R IDs]). Finally, we thank
the reviewers for highlighting [clarity / structure / visualizations]
([R IDs]).
```

这是**句式模板**；槽位只填写 review 实际认可的内容。某一维度没有相应评语就删掉。较短的回复可压缩成 2–3 句，不必凑齐全部维度。

| 功能 | 从原帖归纳的句式 | 怎么填 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 映射编号 | For clarity, we refer to Reviewers [IDs] as R1, R2, and R3, respectively. | 后续各帖保持一致 |
| 总体认可 | We are encouraged by their recognition of the key contributions and strengths of our work. | 下一段用具体归属展开 |
| 研究意义 | In particular, we appreciate the recognition of the importance of [problem] ([R IDs]). | 问题 + 为什么重要 |

## Summary 的共性骨架

**用途：**给 AC 一个能够直接用于综合判断的讨论总结。它可以回顾 reviewers 的认可，但主体要增加“讨论后发生了什么”。截图里的认可、提分、尚未反馈、推荐评价都各有段落功能。

| 顺序 | 你多篇 Summary 中的共同模块 | 应写到什么粒度 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 感谢 AC 主持与投入 | 1–2 句，不重复长篇感谢 |
| B | 重新汇总审稿人认可 | 意义、创新、方法、实验；用 R 编号或少量必要评分归属 |
| C | 概括本轮回应与更新 | 补了什么证据、做了什么澄清，指向 General Response |
| D | 明确的正向讨论结果 | 谁明确提分、认可什么；初始支持与后续提分分开 |
| E | 分 reviewer 记录未收敛问题 | 无反馈、仍有分歧、已接受局限；具体到问题和证据 |
| F | 自信推荐与判断请求 | 贡献 + 已有证据 + 为什么值得正面评价 |
| G | 固定收尾 | 感谢 AC 时间与投入 → 单独一段 “Wishing you a pleasant day ahead.” → 署名 Authors / The Authors（按原帖） |

**与你的 General Response 的对应关系：**认可段是回顾；实验/讨论更新变成一句导航；新增加的是 reviewer 状态和给 AC 的评价请求。普通技术问题的完整论证应已在正式回复中，不靠 AC 私信增加配额。

### 你反复使用的表达及可替换形式

| 功能 | 表达 |
| --- | --- |
| 感谢主持 | We sincerely thank the Area Chairs for their diligent oversight of the review process. |
| 认可回顾 | We appreciate the reviewers' recognition of our research's [significance / novelty / soundness] ([R IDs]). |
| 导航已有更新 | These updates are summarized in our General Response. |
| 明确提分 | We are grateful that [reviewers] raised their ratings following the discussion. |
| 无反馈 | Regarding [reviewer], we have not received further feedback on [issue]. |
| 有剩余分歧 | A disagreement remains about [specific point]; our response is supported by [evidence]. |
| 自信推荐 | We respectfully submit that [contribution and evidence] merit a positive assessment. |
| 综合判断 | We hope the final assessment will consider [key evidence] alongside the discussion record. |
| 你的固定祝福收尾 | Wishing you a pleasant day ahead. |

**五篇共同使用的原句：**LoRAM、FloE、LVSpec、LVLM Survey、HybridKV 的 Summary（FloE 为合并的 General Response & Summary）都使用 **“Wishing you a pleasant day ahead.”**。这是这组案例稳定的个人写作习惯，放在感谢与评价请求之后，单独成段；有署名时，位于署名前。提炼你的共性模板时保留原句，不替换成通用的 “Thank you for your consideration.”。

原帖常见的 “all concerns have been addressed” 可以表示作者已逐项回应；若要表达“审稿人确认已解决”，则必须有对应评论。模板将两者分开，保留积极语气。

### Summary 中争取公平评价的论证套路

你在有争议的 Summary 中，常将**其他 reviewer 的肯定、自己的回应与证据、争议 reviewer 的反馈状态**放在一起，请 AC 综合判断。这里不只是总结进展，也是在为工作争取充分、独立的评估。

### 一 用其他 reviewer 的肯定，回应某一项负面判断

**顺序：明确争议问题 → 引用其他 reviewer 对同一问题的认可 → 给本文证据 → 指出仍需判断的分歧 → 请 AC 独立评估。**

LoRAM 的 Summary 将其他 reviewers 对贡献的认可，与 asc9 对创新性的保留意见放在一起；HybridKV 汇总正面评价、两位 reviewers 的提分和方法实证；Survey 则用技术评价与具体回应争取对整体价值的重新评估。这些是作者在原帖中提出的评价主张，不能只凭支持人数认定争议意见已被推翻。

最有力的引用是**对同一个具体问题的认可**。例如，R1 明确认可某个机制的创新性，就与 R3“只是已有方法”的判断直接相关；R1 只称赞写作清晰，则不能据此否定 R3 的正确性问题。

```text
We ask the AC to consider the differing assessments of [specific issue].
Reviewers [IDs] explicitly recognized [relevant strength], while Reviewer
[ID] maintained that [specific objection]. Our response in [location]
clarifies [distinction] and provides [evidence]. We believe this evidence,
together with the reviewers' observations on the same point, supports
[bounded conclusion]. We respectfully request an independent assessment
of this issue in the final decision.
```

**更坚定的写法：**

```text
The concern that [X] appears inconsistent with [specific evidence] and
with Reviewers [IDs]' recognition of [the same technical point]. We ask
that this concern be checked against [section/table/response], rather than
carried forward without considering the clarification we provided.
```

### 二 对方不再讨论时，强调回应应被纳入判断

你的原帖有将“没有进一步反馈”与“我们认为 concern 已充分回应”放在一起的写法。适合保留的论证是：**问题已经逐项回答 → 证据可核查 → 未收到进一步反驳或残留问题说明 → 请 AC 明确评估回应**。

沉默本身无法区分“认可”“未被说服”“没有时间继续回复”。因此，对外可以坚定地陈述没有收到后续异议，但不替 reviewer 宣布默认接受。这样仍然能争取公平：请 AC 直接判断证据，而非让缺少对话成为作者回应未被考虑的原因。

| 想表达的意思 | 有力度且准确的写法 |
| --- | --- |
| 我们已回答，对方没有进一步指出问题 | We provided a point-by-point response, and have not received further clarification of which concern remains unresolved. |
| 尚未有针对新证据的反驳 | No further objection to this specific evidence has been communicated to us. |
| 请不要忽略我们的回应 | We ask that our response be assessed on its substance, even in the absence of further reviewer engagement. |
| 不要机械沿用原评语 | We request that the original concern be reconsidered in light of the evidence submitted during rebuttal. |
| 请 AC 判断是否仍构成拒稿理由 | We ask the AC to determine whether the stated concern remains a valid basis for the recommendation after considering our clarification. |

```text
We responded to [concern] on [date] with [evidence; location]. We have not
received further feedback identifying any remaining issue with this
response. In our view, the evidence addresses [precise concern]. We ask
that the AC assess this response directly and explain whether the concern
remains material to the final recommendation.
```

这里的 “In our view” 明确是作者判断；若 reviewer 曾明确写 “This resolves my concern”，则可直接报告其认可并引用记录。

### 三 表达几个月研究与大量资源投入值得被认真评估

投入可以解释作者为什么重视评审过程，也可以自然地表达公平诉求。写作上将**研究投入与程序请求**相连，再将**录用或正面评价与贡献及证据**相连。这样既有人味，也能让 AC 知道具体需要做什么。

```text
This work represents several months of sustained research and substantial
computational effort. We have taken the review process seriously and
invested further effort in addressing each concern. We respectfully ask
for a careful and fair assessment of the contribution and the full
discussion record, including the evidence provided in our rebuttal.
```

**更有个人感、语气更直接的版本：**

```text
We care deeply about this work, which has taken several months and
substantial resources to develop and evaluate. We hope that the effort
we have put into both the research and the rebuttal will be met with
careful consideration of the evidence and a fair evaluation of the work.
```

“several months” 和 “substantial resources” 来自作者在本次交流中提供的情况，以上是据此整理的模板，不是五篇原帖的逐字摘录。使用时按具体论文的真实投入填写，无须公开敏感预算。

### 四 合并成 Summary 中的一段公平评价请求

```text
We appreciate Reviewers [IDs]' recognition of [specific contribution].
The remaining concern from Reviewer [ID] is [precise issue], which we
addressed in [response location] through [evidence]. We have not received
further feedback explaining which part of this evidence is insufficient.
In our view, it addresses the stated concern, and we respectfully ask the
AC to assess it directly alongside the other reviewers' observations.

This work represents several months of sustained research and substantial
computational effort. We have also made a serious effort to respond to
every concern during rebuttal. We hope that the final assessment will give
careful consideration to the contribution, the supporting evidence, and
the full discussion record, so that the work receives a fair evaluation.

Wishing you a pleasant day ahead.

The Authors
```

这一段可放在 **Summary 的 reviewer 状态之后、固定祝福之前**。若存在正式违规问题，Summary 可简要指出并定位到专门报告；详细事实与规则仍放在对应入口。

## 跟进与提分后回复

**共性格式：**标题带 Follow-up + Reviewer ID；先接住上一条反馈，再逐项说修改；如果 reviewer 已提分，感谢其重新评估，并确认会落实讨论中承诺的修改。

LoRAM 的 BSMa 对话尤其适合讲：reviewer 给出 large/small 术语和 unchanged weights 的明确要求 → 作者分两项回应及定位 → reviewer 明确提分 → 作者感谢。这里可以学习的是回应的可追踪性。

| 情况 | 写作顺序 | 可用表达 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 对方说明疑问 | 感谢澄清 → 重述真实 concern → 新回答 | Thank you for clarifying. We now understand that your concern is about [X]. |
| 对方建议修改 | 认同合理性 → 改了什么 → 位置 | We agree that clearer terminology will help readers understand [X]. |
| 对方认可/提分 | 感谢重新评价 → 具体修改承诺 | Thank you for the updated assessment. We will incorporate [discussion] into [section]. |
| 对方仍不同意 | 确认分歧 → 精确区别 → 证据 | The remaining distinction is [X], which is evaluated in [location]. |

```text
Title: Follow-up Response to Reviewer [ID]

Thank you for [clarifying the concern / taking another look at our work].

1. [Issue A]
[Answer or completed revision], in [location].

2. [Issue B]
[Answer or revision], supported by [evidence].

We hope these updates address [specific concern] and welcome any remaining
questions.
```

```text
Title: Thank You for the Updated Assessment

Thank you for the updated assessment and for your constructive suggestions.
We appreciate your recognition of [specific aspect, if explicitly stated].
We will incorporate [discussion / analysis / references] into [section]
of the final version, if accepted.

The Authors
```

“Your support means a lot to us” 可用于确有支持时的短感谢。原帖的 “Thanks for your raising score” 可润色为 “Thank you for raising your score” 或 “Thank you for the updated assessment”。

## 催 reviewer 与请 AC 协调

### 催 reviewer 的五段功能

**称呼 → 已回应背景 → 截止提醒 → 询问剩余问题 → 感谢与署名。**你的多篇原帖共享这套结构。标题可写 `Follow-up on [topic] before the Discussion Deadline`；正文写清实际截止时刻与时区，避免标题说一天、正文说两天。

| 原帖常用表达 | 作用 | 更具体的填法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Following up on our recent exchange … | 接续已有对话 | Following up on our response of [date] regarding [topic] … |
| With the discussion deadline approaching … | 提示时间窗口 | With the discussion closing on [exact deadline] … |
| We are eager to address any remaining issues. | 表达愿意继续回应 | We would welcome any remaining questions about [issue]. |
| Please feel free to share any thoughts you may have. | 邀请反馈 | Could you let us know whether [specific concern] remains unresolved? |
| Looking forward to hearing from you! | 人际交流式收尾 | 可保留；不必与多句感谢叠加 |

```text
Title: Follow-up on [Concern] before the Discussion Deadline

Dear Reviewer [ID],

Following up on our response of [date], we would like to check whether any
concern remains about [issue]. Our main clarification is [one sentence],
with the evidence in [response item / table].

With the discussion closing on [date/time/time zone], we would welcome
any further feedback while there is still time for us to respond.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
The Authors
```

### 请 AC 协调的共同格式

**称呼 chairs → 感谢监督 → 已认真回复 → 指明哪位缺反馈 → 请求促进参与 → 说明反馈为何有帮助 → 感谢。**LoRAM、Survey、LVSpec 的 AC 评论都能看到这一顺序。FloE 则进一步列 concern 和回应摘要；AC 后续回复表示已提醒 reviewers。

| 作用 | 可复用表达 |
| --- | --- |
| 已做准备 | We have carefully prepared our rebuttal and responded to [issues]. |
| 当前状态 | We have not yet received feedback from Reviewer [ID] on [point]. |
| 请求协调 | We would appreciate your assistance in encouraging their participation in the discussion. |
| 说明目的 | Their feedback would help us address any remaining concerns before [deadline]. |
| 跟进 AC | We are following up on our confidential comment of [date] concerning [issue]. |

```text
Title: Request for Assistance with the Reviewer Discussion

Dear Area Chair,

Thank you for overseeing the review process. We submitted our response
to Reviewer [ID] on [date], and it became available to reviewers on [date].
We have not yet received feedback on [specific issue; response location].

As the discussion closes on [deadline], we would appreciate your assistance
in inviting feedback on this point. This would help us address any remaining
concerns within the available response window.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
The Authors
```

催 AC 时补上“此前何时发过、何种程序问题、需要什么指导”，不要把普通 Summary 没收到单独回复视为必须催促的理由。提醒频率与次数服从会议规则；不把历史上的多次催审当成固定流程。

## 反馈问题与举报的共性格式

**普通保密反馈**用于提醒 AC 关注关键事实或需要协调的问题；**正式 Review Issue Report**使用指定表单及当前类别；**严重违规报告**使用指定诚信渠道。标题与表单类型分开看。

你的 Survey 报告使用类别 + Justification；LVSpec 标题为 Review Issue Report 的帖子实际采用 Author–Editor Confidential Comment，逐条对应 W1–W3 与原稿位置。共性是 **问题 → 证据 → 影响 → 请求**。

### 六部分逐项填写

| 部分 | 要写清的内容 | 有用表达 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 身份与对象 | paper ID、reviewer ID、报告类别 | We are writing to report a [factual/procedural] issue concerning Reviewer [ID]. |
| 事件 | review 原话、首次出现时间、是否可回应 | The review states that [X]. / The concern was first raised on [date]. |
| 反证或记录 | 原稿 Table/Section、作者回复、具体时间 | However, the original submission provides [evidence] in [location]. |
| 实质影响 | 错误如何影响推荐或回应机会 | This matters because the recommendation relies on [premise]. |
| 处理请求 | 核查、纠正、转交、回应机会、独立判断 | We ask that this point be checked against [record]. |
| 收尾 | 提供材料、感谢、署名 | Please let us know if further documentation is needed. |

### 坚定但有依据的表达

- **指出事实错误：**“The claim that [X] is factually incorrect: [evidence].”
- **指出遗漏回应：**“The review still relies on [premise], which our response on [date] addresses.”
- **要求依据：**“Could the reviewer clarify which remaining concern supports the overall assessment?”
- **指出无回应机会：**“This left us no opportunity to address the new concern through the normal process.”
- **要求纠正：**“We request a correction of the record and an assessment based on the corrected facts.”
- **请求核查可靠性：**“We ask the AC to assess the reliability of this review in light of the documented discrepancies.”
- **举报可能违规：**“We are reporting a potential violation of [policy] and request an investigation through the designated process.”

### 完整模板

```text
Title: Review Issue Report — Reviewer [ID] — [Specific Issue]

Dear [AC / Designated Chair],

We are writing to report a [factual / procedural / potential policy] issue
concerning Reviewer [ID] and Submission [paper ID].

1. Observed issue
The review states [brief accurate quotation/paraphrase] in [location].
[If applicable: This was first posted on date/time, after deadline X.]

2. Relevant evidence
The original submission provides [evidence] in [section/table/page].
We clarified this in [response item] on [date]. As of [date], [observable
status of the review or discussion].

3. Impact on the assessment
This discrepancy matters because [specific effect on the recommendation
or on our opportunity to respond].

4. Requested action
We request [verification/correction/authorized opportunity to respond/
independent assessment/investigation under the applicable policy].

Thank you for considering this report. We can provide [records] if needed.
The Authors of Submission [ID]
```

**怎样加强说服力：**把原话和证据位置写准，比增加 unfair / unreliable / unqualified 更有效。高技术分与低 overall 可以请求解释；少数低分、低 confidence、符号错误本身不能证明失职或 LLM 违规。迟到 review 可能是 emergency review，优先请求实际回应机会。

## 自信推荐与决定后回应

### 推荐工作的四层表达

| 层次 | 内容 | 句式 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 贡献 | 我们做到了什么 | Our central contribution is [X], which enables [Y] under [constraint]. |
| 证据 | 为什么能这样主张 | This is supported by [comparison/ablation/analysis]. |
| 受众价值 | 对谁有用 | We believe this provides a practical option for [users] facing [constraint]. |
| 评价请求 | 为什么值得正面评价 | Taken together, [contribution] and [evidence] make a strong case for acceptance. |

FloE 的例子把推荐落到资源受限用户；HybridKV 的 Summary 把方法、实证、已有认可汇成正面评价请求。推荐可自信，但不要把作者判断写成所有 reviewer 的立场。

```text
We remain confident in the contribution of this work. It enables
[capability] under [constraint], supported by [specific evidence].
For [target users], this provides [practical benefit]. With the clarification
of [main concern] and the acknowledged limitation of [boundary], we believe
the work merits a positive assessment on the strength of this contribution.
```

### Response to Meta-review 的格式

**感谢 AC 认可 → 对照剩余建议 → 逐项落实承诺与位置 → 重申贡献及证据 → 正面评价请求。**这与 ARR discussion Summary 是不同阶段，使用 commitment 表单时尤其要区分。

```text
We thank the Area Chair for recognizing [specific contribution].
We will address the remaining suggestions as follows:

1. [Suggestion A]: We will [concrete revision] in [section].
2. [Suggestion B]: We will [concrete revision] in [section].

Our central contribution remains [X], supported by [evidence]. We believe
these revisions will improve the presentation and usefulness of the work.
Thank you for your consideration.
```

若需申诉，使用会议规定的程序窗口与理由；模板库附录保留迟到 review、严重违规、AC 问题和决定后申诉的独立模板。
